# Pihulka Check Support

Pihulka Check reminds you to take your medications and repeats the reminder until you respond.

## Contact us

Write to **[{{ site.support_email }}](mailto:{{ site.support_email }})** in English or Ukrainian.

If something isn't working, please include:

- your iPhone model and iOS version;
- the app version (you'll find it in the app's settings);
- what you did and what happened.

## Frequently asked questions

### Reminders don't arrive

- **Allow notifications.** In iOS Settings, open Notifications → Pihulka Check and check that notifications are turned on.
- **Allow Time Sensitive notifications.** Reminders then get through Focus modes and Do Not Disturb.
- **Open the app at least once every few days.** iOS lets an app keep only a limited number of reminders scheduled in advance. Pihulka Check tops them up every time you open it, and warns you if they're running low.
- **Silent mode.** When your iPhone is in silent mode, reminders arrive without sound.

### How do I stop the repeats?

Respond to the reminder: mark the dose as taken, snooze it or skip it. Once you respond, the repeats for that dose stop.

If you don't respond, the reminder repeats (by default once more, an hour later). After that, the dose is marked as missed with no response. You can change the repeat interval and duration in the app's settings, including for each medication separately.

### Do I need the internet?

No. Your phone schedules the reminders itself, so they arrive without the internet. You only need a connection to sync, if you have an account.

### What happens when I travel?

Your schedule follows your phone's local time. A dose at 08:00 reminds you at 08:00 wherever you are.

### Do I need an account?

No, everything essential works without one. An account gives you two things:

- a backup, so your schedule and history aren't lost when you reinstall the app;
- sync between your phones.

You can create an account in the app, in the Account section.

### How do I move my data to a new phone?

Install Pihulka Check and sign in: your medications, schedules and history will come back.

Without an account, your data is stored only on your phone. Once you delete the app, it can't be recovered.

### I forgot my password

Reset it from the sign-in screen. We'll email you a six-digit code that's valid for 15 minutes.

### How do I delete my account?

In the app: "Account" → "Delete account". Your data is removed from the server immediately and from backups within 7 days. See the [privacy policy](../privacy/) for details.

## Medical disclaimer

Pihulka Check only reminds you to take doses on the schedule you enter yourself. It doesn't give medical advice and doesn't replace a doctor or pharmacist. Don't change your treatment without talking to your doctor.
